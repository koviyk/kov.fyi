About Me

I'm an artist and designer residing in Israel. As of recent I've been pouring more time into making music, taking my time to figure it out until I buy the necessary tools to fuel my hobby. Between 25'–26' I studied to be a Practical Engineer of Communication Design, and I'm currently working in an unrelated part time job while trying to expand my portfolio.

I consider myself an autodidact, constantly striving to learn new things. When I'm not learning or working I try to find time to play video games, or go on walks.

Although I love the internet and what it has to offer, I do my best to disconnect from it, as the majority of people on social media's mindset and opinions do not align with mine. I am not interested in forming online friendships, and I really value my real life connections. More
